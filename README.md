### Product leader · AI-first enterprise SaaS · PhD

I started out as an engineer and have spent 19 years building software products, most of that time in product management. Today I lead product for an enterprise mobile portfolio at a global enterprise software company, where my work centers on AI and agent features: what they should do for a customer, and the evaluation and guardrails that decide whether they are fit to ship.

I also build my own tools with coding agents, including a portfolio tracker that runs on my own investing rules. That is where most of my opinions about where AI genuinely helps, and where it doesn't, come from.

### The book

**[AI-Powered Product Management](https://link.springer.com/book/9798868829369)** (Apress, October 2026) is a practical guide for aspiring and early-career product managers on using AI across the whole job: research, strategy, specs, launch, growth, and landing the role in the first place.
